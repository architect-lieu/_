On the second visit to Tinos.

Six months later, the wall had been re-painted. The painter was not the same painter. The colour was three percent yellower. I had been holding the picture in my head all winter, in a slightly different shade. Both shades were correct.

N. 1322 March 2026Studio

Why I went back to film, again.

Briefly: because the digital file is finished the moment it is made, and I am almost never finished with a picture the moment it is made. Film is a deferral. Deferral is, increasingly, where I want to live.

An afternoon with E., who has been a friend long enough that we should have made a portrait by now and have not. We talked instead, for three hours, and made tea, and lit the lamp at five, and looked at her grandfather's contact sheets, and did not photograph anything.

N. 1119 January 2026Argolid

Three rolls in the Argolid.

Up at four. Cold enough to feel surprised by it. The light came at six fifty and lasted nine minutes longer than I'd planned for. Two of the three rolls were of the same ridge, photographed twice from the same hill, eleven minutes apart.
